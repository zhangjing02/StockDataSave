Additional topics include emerging cybersecurity threats, the increasing pace of crypto-related attacks, incident response challenges, and how security teams are adapting to an environment where threats can develop in minutes rather than days.

The interview also explores Blockaid's preventative security approach, including transaction simulation technology designed to identify suspicious activity and potential risks before transactions are executed on-chain.

Looking ahead, Ben-Natan expects organizations across the digital asset ecosystem to place greater emphasis on proactive security measures, automated threat detection, transaction monitoring, and real-time response capabilities as cyber threats continue to evolve.
